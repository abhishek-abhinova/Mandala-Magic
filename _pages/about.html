The Practice

One Circle, Every Morning

In 1999, Orchid made a simple promise to herself: draw one mandala a day as a way of naming what she hoped the day could be. No audience, no product, no plan.

Twenty-seven years later, over nine thousand circles have accumulated — a quiet lifetime of intentions. What she never expected was that the world would want them, wear them, and hang them on their walls.

Inspiration

Where the Magic Lives

Orchid finds most of her art in the spaces between things: the hush before sunrise, the weight of a word she hasn't said, the way light falls on water. Her digital landscapes are imagined places; her mandalas are feelings that learned to draw.

And occasionally, a whole universe arrives all at once — those become the pieces you'll find in the Cosmic Dreams collection.
